Across my research experiences, I have been interested in understanding how differences in neural organization, cognitive processes, and experiential factors contribute to individual variability in learning and behavior.

## Ongoing Projects

### Mathematical Cognition and Neural Plasticity
`EEG/ERP` `Mathematical Cognition` `Individual Differences`

**Core Question:** How does neural processing during arithmetic verification vary across individuals?

**Current Focus:** Examining how mathematical proficiency and long-term experience influence neural efficiency during arithmetic processing.

<details>
<summary>Project Details</summary>

Using EEG/ERP, this project investigates how individual differences in mathematical proficiency relate to neural activity during multiplication and function verification. Current analyses focus on posterior P300 activity, neural efficiency, and whether group differences in neural responses can be explained by baseline mathematical aptitude.

**Methods:** EEG/ERP, behavioral assessment, mediation analysis  
**Software:** MATLAB, EEGLAB, ERPLAB, JASP

</details>

### Memory Encoding and Recognition in Children
`fMRI` `Exercise Intervention` `Learning & Memory`

**Core Question:** Can acute aerobic exercise alter neural systems supporting memory encoding and recognition in children with obesity?

**Current Focus:** Examining whether exercise-induced changes in behavioral performance, structural brain regions, and functional activation patterns contribute to memory processing.

<details>
<summary>Project Details</summary>

This project investigates memory encoding and recognition in children with obesity before and after acute aerobic exercise. We examine whether behavioral performance and brain measures, including structural and functional changes in regions such as the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex, are associated with memory-related outcomes.

**Methods:** fMRI, structural MRI, behavioral assessment  
**Software:** SPM, FSL, FreeSurfer

</details>

## Selected Previous Research Experiences

### Language and Face Lateralization in Autism Spectrum Disorder
`fTCD` `Neural Organization` `Atypical Development`

**Core Question:** How does neural organization for language and face processing differ in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD)?

**Key Finding:** Typically developing children around age 10 already showed a trend toward left-lateralized language processing, whereas age-matched children with ASD showed a right-lateralized tendency. In contrast, face lateralization appeared relatively stable across groups, suggesting that neural specialization for face processing may emerge earlier than language specialization.

**Output:** Poster presented at the *Asia Pacific Society of Speech, Language and Hearing (APSSLH) Conference*, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

### Sensorimotor Profiles in Developmental Language Disorder
`Eye-Tracking` `Clinical Assessment` `Language Development`

**Core Question:** How are sensorimotor processes associated with language development in children with developmental language disorder (DLD)?

**My Role:** Implemented eye-tracking paradigms and administered Wechsler Intelligence Scale assessments, reading and language measures, and other standardized tests to characterize individual differences in sensorimotor and language development.

**Contribution:** Participated in projects that resulted in peer-reviewed publications examining motor, proprioceptive, and haptic functioning in Mandarin-speaking children with DLD.

### Language Anxiety Among Exchange Students
`Language Learning` `Individual Differences` `Cross-Cultural Research`

**Core Question:** How is language anxiety related to language achievement among Mandarin-speaking exchange students?

**Key Finding:** Students reporting the highest levels of language anxiety did not necessarily demonstrate the lowest language achievement. Relationships between anxiety and performance varied across listening, speaking, reading, and writing, highlighting substantial individual variability in second-language learning outcomes.

**Output:** Poster presented at the *International Conference on Educational Innovation (ICEI)*, Hsinchu, Taiwan.

### Numerical Cognition and Distance Effect
`Mathematical Cognition` `Behavioral Research` `Experimental Design`

**Core Question:** How do numerical representations and task strategies influence arithmetic processing?

**Contribution:** Independently designed a PsychoPy-based experimental paradigm and collected behavioral data from 50 university students.

**Impact:** This project sparked my interest in rigorous experimental design and revealed how individuals may adopt distinct cognitive strategies when solving the same numerical task, leading to substantial variation in response time and accuracy.
